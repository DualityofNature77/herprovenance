About

Home for the woman who carries the legacy.

Her Provenance is for socially active, spiritually aware Black women, including women of mixed ancestry who identify as Black women.

What Her Provenance is

A home for knowledge, uplift and tools. We tell the history of the Black women who shaped nations, teach the patterns that hold us back at work and how to answer them, explore the spiritual traditions that have always sustained our activism, and build The Record, a private app that keeps your name on your work.

Who’s behind it

Idara Whem founded Her Provenance and wrote The Provenance Files and the Sacred Balance Library. Her Provenance is part of The Provenance, the social-activism home of Sieber Fillman Publishing, an independent press in Tucson, Arizona. Its spiritual practice lives at TMBOL.org.

What we believe

  • Black women have always shown up. The record should say so.
  • Excellence is inherited. Knowing your history is knowing your worth.
  • Spirit and action belong together.
  • How we treat each other is part of the movement.
  • Your data is yours. We build tools that can’t read what you record.

Contact

For press, speaking, partnerships, bulk book orders, or a woman you think belongs in Her Legacy, send a note. We reply within two business days.
